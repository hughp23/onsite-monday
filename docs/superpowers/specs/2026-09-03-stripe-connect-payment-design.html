How Onsite Monday will handle payments between job posters and tradespeople at launch

Date: 3 September 2026 Audience: Founders Approved

Overview

This document describes how Onsite Monday will handle payments between job posters and tradespeople. It replaces the original escrow approach with a simpler, cheaper model built on Stripe — a payment provider we already use for subscriptions.

The model is called Protected Payments. It works like escrow from the user's perspective — the poster's money is held securely until the job is done and reviewed — but it avoids the regulatory and cost burden of running a formal escrow service.

Important terminology: We do not call this "escrow" in our terms or marketing. Escrow is a regulated term in the UK requiring FCA authorisation. What we offer is functionally identical — held funds, released on completion — but Stripe holds the funds and the regulatory licence, not us. This means Onsite Monday does not need its own FCA authorisation for this payment model.

Why We Changed Approach

The original plan used Mangopay, a specialist escrow and e-money platform. Mangopay is well-suited for large, mature marketplaces but carries meaningful upfront cost that is hard to absorb before we have volume. Stripe Connect achieves the same outcome at a fraction of the cost:

Stripe is already integrated into the app for subscription billing, so extending it to handle job payments means one vendor, one dashboard, and significantly less complexity to build and maintain at launch.

What Is "Protected Payments"?

Protected Payments is our name for the experience we offer users. In plain terms:

  • Job posters pay when they confirm a hire. Their money is held securely by Stripe — a regulated financial institution — and is not released to the tradesperson until the job is complete and a review has been submitted.
  • Tradespeople know the money is sitting ready before they show up. Once the job is done and reviewed, it is transferred to their account and paid out within an agreed window.
  • The platform retains a small percentage fee automatically on every job payment, in addition to subscription revenue.

Regulatory & Compliance

Who holds the money?

Stripe. At no point does Onsite Monday hold funds directly. Stripe is an FCA-authorised e-money institution operating in the UK. They are the custodian of all funds between payment and payout.

What does Onsite Monday need to do?

  • Accept Stripe's platform terms (already done for subscriptions)
  • Ensure our terms of service clearly describe the payment flow to users
  • Ensure job posters and tradespeople each accept Stripe's connected account agreement during sign-up
  • Support Strong Customer Authentication (SCA/3DS2) — Stripe handles this automatically in our checkout flow

What do we NOT need?

  • Our own FCA registration or e-money licence
  • A separate regulated escrow account
  • A formal escrow agent

Identity verification (KYC & KYB)

Stripe handles all identity verification automatically through its hosted onboarding flow, which we embed directly in the app. We do not build or manage any verification ourselves.

  • Individual tradesperson — Stripe verifies name, date of birth, address, and bank account details
  • Sole trader or limited company — Stripe also verifies business registration details and directors

Stripe owns this process and takes on the associated compliance burden.

What the platform earns

On every job payment, the platform retains a percentage fee before transferring the remainder to the tradesperson. This happens automatically — Stripe transfers less than the amount charged, and the difference stays in our Stripe balance. This is separate from, and in addition to, subscription revenue.

Platform transaction fee: TBD — to be decided by founders. Typical marketplace transaction fees range from 5–15%.

User Experience

Job Poster

  1. Browses the jobs board and hires a tradesperson
  2. Enters card details in the app — payment taken immediately on hire confirmation
  3. Receives confirmation that funds are securely held
  4. When the job is done, marks it complete and submits a mandatory review
  5. Sees confirmation that the tradesperson has been paid

Tradesperson

  1. Completes Stripe's identity verification once during sign-up (a few minutes, guided by Stripe)
  2. Applies for and accepts jobs as normal
  3. Can see in the app that payment is secured before starting work
  4. Completes the job on site
  5. Receives funds in their account within the payout window after the poster submits a review
  6. Views earnings history and payout schedule in their account dashboard

Account Structure

Both subscription payments and job payments run through the same Stripe account — no separation needed. Stripe tracks them separately by type and they appear distinctly in the dashboard.

Open Questions for Founders

  • Platform transaction fee % To be set before launch. Affects tradesperson take-home pay and our unit economics. Typical range: 5–15%. This is in addition to subscription revenue.
  • Payout window How many days after job completion and review submission does the tradesperson receive their money? Recommendation: launch with one fixed window (e.g. 7 or 14 days). Tier-based speed differences (Bronze slower, Gold faster) can be added later as a product differentiator.
  • Dispute handling If a job poster disputes a card charge after marking a job complete, Stripe's dispute process applies. We need a clear policy for how the platform mediates between the two parties.
  • Cancellation & refunds What happens if a job is cancelled after payment is taken but before work starts? A clear refund policy is needed before launch.
